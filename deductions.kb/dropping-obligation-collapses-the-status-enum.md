Once obligation stops being a stored status, the enum has nothing left
to hold that is not either fiat or absence.

`obligation-is-derived-not-stored.md` removes `obligated(ROUTE)` on the
general principle that standing which can be computed must not also be
stored, or the two can disagree. That principle does not stop at
`obligated`. Apply it to the rest of ACS's four and they fall the same
way: `proven`/`certified` is read off a premise chain or a named check
at point of use, so storing it is the same duplication; `described` was
never a status, just unlabeled prose. What survives is `stipulated` —
warrant by fiat, which nothing computes because it is a decision — and
the residue, an assertion warranted only by consistency with the
admitted set. Two cells.

This is why an `asserted` claim and an `open` claim turn out to be one
thing under two names, and why the sigil set has exactly as many cells
as there are base statuses rather than one per status word.

Recorded as a deduction rather than a `depends:` edge because it is
support, and support between claims goes through the spine — the rule
this realm is proposing and therefore owes. The edge it replaces was
this graph's last claim → claim `depends:`.
