# `questions.kb/` kept, but the axioms do not imply it

**Applied — as a keep, flagged rather than smuggled.**

**The problem.** {TL, RN} implies three collections: claims,
deductions, sources. Not five. Worse than "not implied," this realm's
own `../../../claims.kb/two-base-statuses-not-four.md` says an open
claim *is* effectively a question — so the axioms arguably say the two
collections should be one.

**Why I kept it anyway.** At repo weight files are cheap, and "how
should repo weight absorb TL/RN" does not restate as a proposition
without distortion. Forcing it into claim form buys conformity and
costs legibility.

**But that is a decision, not a derivation**, which is why
`../../../repo-weight-derivation.md` says so in its own voice under
"What the axioms do not imply." **Needs fiat.**

`definitions.kb/` is the same shape and less contentious: a definition
is not truth-apt, so the axioms simply do not reach it. Keep on
independent grounds.

**What to check.** Whether you want the merge. If open claims and
questions really are one thing, saying so would be the strongest
simplification available — and the derivation would get shorter, not
longer.
