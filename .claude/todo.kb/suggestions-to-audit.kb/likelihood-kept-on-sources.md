# `likelihood` deliberately kept on sources

**Applied — as an exception.** `sources.jsonschema.yaml` keeps
`likelihood`; only claims and deductions lost it.

**Why.** Sources are not members of the accepted set. They are
provenance: a transcript, a paper, a participant. "How much do I trust
this recollection" is a genuinely different question from "what
warrants this claim," and TL does not reach it — TL governs what may
enter the ledger, and a source never enters, it only attests.

**What to check.** Whether that line is real or whether I drew it to
justify touching less. The honest worry: if source likelihood ever
propagates into how much a claim is believed, it is doing warrant's job
under another name, and it should go the same way as the others. It
currently propagates nowhere, which is why I left it — but "propagates
nowhere" is also a description of dead weight.
