The dynamics TL cannot absorb (chat.md:722): the accepted set is
revised under attack — refuted claims are retracted (propagating), open
obligations are actively driven toward discharge or promotion-to-axiom,
last revision wins. Independent of TL: a system satisfying TL vacuously
(everything a declared axiom) is the "perfectly labeled graveyard"
countermodel. Prior art: AGM belief revision fused with Lakatos's
proofs-and-refutations dialectic.
