The ladder's middle rung: claims held as files in a `.kb/` tree —
`Skill(llm-discourse-graph)` and `Skill(llm-kb)` — rather than in a
conversation (chat weight) or a typed kernel (mechanized). Named by
parallel with "chat weight": the defining property is the medium. A
claim at repo weight survives the session that made it, is addressable
by path, and is readable by every agent in every later session.

This is the rung the operator's daily work actually accumulates on,
and — per `../claims.kb/repo-weight-rung-is-unbuilt.md` — the one that
has absorbed the least of {TL, RN}.
