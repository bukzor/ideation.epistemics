# trace-sim.kb -- maintenance guide

`../trace-sim.md` defines this theory and stipulates its words.

## What belongs here

One claim per file about the event-trace simulation of carriers and junctures, with no owner in the loop, confined to the words `../trace-sim.md` and
its containers admit.

## What does NOT belong here

A claim that needs the owner modeled: that is another theory's, and a claim needing its words imports
it through `why:`.

## When

Add a file when a claim in this vocabulary is made, contested, or ruled.
