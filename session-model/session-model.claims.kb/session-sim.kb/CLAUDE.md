# session-sim.kb -- maintenance guide

`../session-sim.md` defines this theory and stipulates its words.

## What belongs here

One claim per file about the full simulated session with a hidden owner and a reply policy, confined to the words `../session-sim.md` and
its containers admit.

## What does NOT belong here

A claim that trace-sim can hold without the owner: that is another theory's, and a claim needing its words imports
it through `why:`.

## When

Add a file when a claim in this vocabulary is made, contested, or ruled.
