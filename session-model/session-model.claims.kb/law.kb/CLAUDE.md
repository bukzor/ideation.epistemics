# law.kb -- maintenance guide

`../law.md` defines this theory and stipulates its words.

## What belongs here

One claim per file about rules as executable objects and how they are checked, confined to the words `../law.md` and
its containers admit.

## What does NOT belong here

A claim about how a rule reaches an agent: that is another theory's, and a claim needing its words imports
it through `why:`.

## When

Add a file when a claim in this vocabulary is made, contested, or ruled.
