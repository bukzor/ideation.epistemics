# problem -- why a model of ledger dynamics exists at all

The owner keeps many claim ledgers, and review of the mature ones had
become unworkable: the cost scaled with the ledger, the payoff did not,
and confidence in the ledgers' grounding went with it. The claims here
diagnose that; every later theory is a response to them.
