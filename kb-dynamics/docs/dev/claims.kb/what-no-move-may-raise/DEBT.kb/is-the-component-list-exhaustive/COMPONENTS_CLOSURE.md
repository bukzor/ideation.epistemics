and the owner expects the exploration to close the list rather than a
ruling:

Open: whether a kind of rot exists that no component counts. The
witness is a ledger the owner distrusts whose vector reads zero, and it
cannot come from the sandbox, since `ATOM_CONTENT` strips the content
that conflict and near-duplicate rot live in; it is a field measurement
over the fleet's ledgers (`SANDBOX_VS_FIELD`). The question's other
half, whether zero on every component leaves every claim trusted, is
closed by `QUEUE_EMPTY_TRUST`: an empty queue entails it, and the
converse fails.
