# The missing capability is a review that is prioritized, and deferred without being dismissed

Every reviewable move and every proposed claim enters a queue ordered by
what rests on it; an item the owner does not reach is deferred, not
dropped, and returns when its weight rises. This is the pathway
`DRIFT_ENTRY` found absent for derivations that become load-bearing
late, and the headline the owner set:

> [!@bukzor] f65fbdf3#L815
> prioritization (and deferral without dismissal) of such reviews I think is the headline missing capability.

`QUEUE` is the read side of it; deferral is the state an unreached item
keeps, and dismissal is a ruling only the owner makes.
