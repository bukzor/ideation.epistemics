# DEBT.kb -- maintenance guide

`../DEBT.md` defines this theory.

## What belongs here

A property of the debt function (what is wanted, what is repayable, how
the queue orders), a candidate rule with what it permits or obliges and
which component it targets, or a guard on an unasked move. A kind of rot
itself goes in `the-kinds-of-rot/COMPONENTS.kb/`.

## What does NOT belong here

How a rule is priced: `../../weighing-a-candidate-rule/RULE_PRICING.md`. Whether a rule is necessary:
the harness shows that (`../../what-the-harness-shows/HARNESS.md`).
