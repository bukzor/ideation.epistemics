# An example is a state, the move the owner ruled on it, and the address

An example is a state, the move the owner ruled on it, and the address of
the ruling; the example store is the observed part of what the owner
holds. Here examples are the recorded bad and good states drawn from real
ledgers with their provenance, and the traces whose expected debt
trajectory the owner has confirmed. Outer argument not restated.
