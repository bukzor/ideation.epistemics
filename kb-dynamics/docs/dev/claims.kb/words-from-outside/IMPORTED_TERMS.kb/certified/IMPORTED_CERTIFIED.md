# A certified claim is warranted by a named, re-runnable check

A certified claim is warranted by a named, re-runnable check; at repo
weight the warrant is a `certified:` field naming the check. Here it is
the ground evidence supplies, and the harness's own properties are what certify the
claims about the harness. Outer argument not restated.
