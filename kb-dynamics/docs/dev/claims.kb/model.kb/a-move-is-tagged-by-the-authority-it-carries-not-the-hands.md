# A move is tagged by the authority it carries, not by the hands that make it

A move made by an agent on the owner's word carries the owner's
authority and cites its license: the address of the ruling, or of the
standing rule that pre-authorizes it. A move with no license is the
agent's own. Rules gate by authority, so "only the owner settles
wording" refuses the agent's own settling and admits the agent's
settling on the owner's review of the draft.

Two kinds of license: per move, an address; standing, a stipulated rule,
of which `UNASKED_MOVES` is the first. The harness's rules are the
default standing license, what an unlicensed move may do, and the
move log (`MOVE_LOG`) records the license with the move. The earlier
tag, "who makes it", is withdrawn on this ruling.
