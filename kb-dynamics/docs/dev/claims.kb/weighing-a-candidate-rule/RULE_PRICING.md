# rule-pricing -- how a candidate rule is represented so its cost and benefit are separately estimable

A proposed change to agent guidance is represented so that its costs, in tokens and in
owner attention, can be weighed against its benefit in debt prevented or
removed. The claims here fix that representation, where its numbers
come from, and what "minimal" means once they exist. This is the
field-side theory: its records belong beside the must-read tooling, and
its instruments are deferred until the hand tally stops scaling.
