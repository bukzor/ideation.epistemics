# Owner attention is dearer than tokens; both convert to dollars, the owner's hour at $150

The two currencies of `CHECK_SETS_COST` compare on one scale the owner
sets, and the scale fixes only the direction: a minute of the owner's
attention outweighs a great many tokens. The rate is highly sensitive
and the owner's to set (`IMPORTED_WEEKLY_COST`). The scale today is
dollars: tokens at their market price, owner attention at the owner's
own rate, initially $150 per hour.
