# RULE_PRICING.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

The rule record's fields, a cost or benefit term and where it is
measured, the minimality condition, and the instruments (replay, log)
that would compute them.

## What does NOT belong here

A particular rule: `../../what-no-move-may-raise/DEBT.md`. When to build an instrument:
`../../the-order-of-work/METHOD.md`.
