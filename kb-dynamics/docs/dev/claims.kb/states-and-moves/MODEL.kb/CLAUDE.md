# MODEL.kb -- maintenance guide

`../MODEL.md` defines this theory.

## What belongs here

A commitment about the shape of a claim, a state, or a move: a field, a
value, a computed quantity, the move table, or the discipline for adding
a state.

## What does NOT belong here

What a rule permits or obliges, and what debt counts: `../../what-no-move-may-raise/DEBT.md`. How
the harness represents content: `../../what-the-harness-shows/HARNESS.md`.
