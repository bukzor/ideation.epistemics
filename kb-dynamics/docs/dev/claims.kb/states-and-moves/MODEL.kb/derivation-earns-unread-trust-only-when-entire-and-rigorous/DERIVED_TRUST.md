# A derivation earns unread trust only when it rests entirely on the owner's claims, rigorously

Not unequivocally. A `why:` arrow denotes grounding; it does not
establish it, and a ground can be necessary without being sufficient.

> [!@bukzor] f65fbdf3#L815
> an agent derivation whose grounding **entirely rests** on my own claims **in a rigorously correct way** earn my trust without reading. But those two additional criteria are real tricky.

So `EFFECTIVE_BASIS`'s fold is a necessary condition for trust and not
the trust itself. What the two criteria demand of the record is
`GROUND_RECORD`; until the record can carry them, a derived claim is
trusted for building on and presented as the agent's reading.
