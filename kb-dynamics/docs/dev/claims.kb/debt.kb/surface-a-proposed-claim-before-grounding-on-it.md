# Before grounding a new claim on a proposed one, surface it for stipulation

The move that first grounds on a `proposed` claim is obliged to surface
that claim to the owner. It moves review to the moment it is cheapest:
one claim, with the context that makes it meaningful already loaded,
rather than N ledgers cold. This is the rule that interrupts
`DRIFT_MECHANISM`. No harness property demands it, since the debt it
prevents is visible and repayable; it grounds on `OBJECTIVE`'s second
goal, cost, and is priced in the field, where it is the one most likely
to be tested first against a real ledger's history.
