# A load-bearing claim whose effective basis is proposed

A claim rests on a proposed basis when the fold over its grounds reaches
an agent claim no one has ruled on. Its contribution scales with what
rests on it. The owner's ranking of where distrust enters puts this kind
first (`DRIFT_ENTRY`), with the refinement that such claims are seldom
ungrounded outright: they are grounded too strongly on an inference from
an owner remark, or on a poor inference later read as ground truth.
