# A claim contradicted by a later owner ruling that does not cite it

A ruling lands on one claim; the claims that rested on the old reading
keep it. The owner named the future form of this kind: dependents that
no longer match an import that moved. Found by comparing a claim's text
against rulings newer than it on the claims it grounds on; prioritized
by what rests on the stale one.
