# A load-bearing claim on a proposed basis

An agent guess no one ruled on, with two derivations stacked on it. The
fold over either derivation's grounds reaches the guess, so both are
effectively proposed whatever the agent called them; the ruling that
repays the debt lands on the guess, and its contribution is what rests
on it.
