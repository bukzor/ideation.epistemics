Global must-read protocol (`~/.claude/must-read.kb/when/evaluating-a-
contested-or-subjective-position.md`), triggered before endorsing any
contested claim: three roles (Advocate steelmans, Skeptic attacks,
Arbiter checks Skeptic didn't fold early) debate to a verdict, with
claims tagged in short ALLCAP labels for back-reference — TL's labeling
discipline, pre-dating the axiom. "If you changed your position,
propagate the correction" is RN's revise-under-attack dynamic, stated
as a standing instruction rather than a store operation.

Gap: no dependency edges — "propagate the correction" is manual prose
("where else might those assumptions have affected your reasoning?"),
not RP's edge-following invalidation, so nothing guarantees every
affected downstream claim is actually found. No status lattice: a
claim is "validated" or silently dropped, not placed in TL's tripartite
{certificate, obligation, axiom}. Scoped to one trigger (contested
endorsement), not a standing ledger.
