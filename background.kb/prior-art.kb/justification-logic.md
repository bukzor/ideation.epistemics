Formulas annotated `t:P` — "t is a justification of P" — explicit
evidence terms replacing bare modal necessity (chat's KS,
`chat.md:728`). TL is "essentially" this: no acceptance without a
statable justification.

Gap: no revision dynamics — evidence terms accumulate; nothing
retracts. The RN half is absent, which is exactly the fusion whose
novelty `../../questions.kb/is-tl-rn-fusion-novel.md` adjudicates.

In this realm: `../../claims.kb/warrant-by-field-presence.md` is `t:P`
spelled for a filesystem — `stipulated:`/`certified:` name the term,
the body is the formula. The one addition is the empty case: evidence
terms accumulate here too, but absence has to be a legal, cheap state.

Verify: Artemov's "The Logic of Justification" (2008 survey); check
whether any justification-logic extension handles retraction.
