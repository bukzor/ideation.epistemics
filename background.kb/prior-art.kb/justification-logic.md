Formulas annotated `t:P` — "t is a justification of P" — explicit
evidence terms replacing bare modal necessity (chat's KS,
`chat.md:728`). TL is "essentially" this: no acceptance without a
statable justification.

Gap: no revision dynamics — evidence terms accumulate; nothing
retracts. The RN half is absent, which is exactly the fusion whose
novelty `../../questions.kb/is-tl-rn-fusion-novel.md` adjudicates.
