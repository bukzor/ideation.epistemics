Postulates for rational revision of belief sets under new,
possibly-contradicting information — RN's "the accepted set is revised
under attack" formalized (chat's KS, `chat.md:728`; also cited in
`../../definitions.kb/refinement-norm.md`).

Gap: no evidence terms — beliefs are bare propositions; nothing records
*why* a belief is held, so revision can't follow justification edges.
The dual gap to justification logic's.

Verify: AGM 1985 ("On the Logic of Theory Change"); check the
literature for justification-aware AGM variants (bears directly on
`../../questions.kb/is-tl-rn-fusion-novel.md`).
