# Mission — why this realm exists

**The operator's productivity is apex.** Everything here is a means to
it. Sound epistemics and fleet coherence are the means — extremely
high-leverage ones if the bets pay out, but means.

The bet: an operator running 10–20 parallel agent sessions a day is
bottlenecked on *trusting what the fleet believes*. Unlabeled
confidence, sycophancy, and context amnesia each cost real rework.
{TL, RN} is the wager that those failures are defeatable
structurally — by what a claim store makes representable — rather
than by staying vigilant.

## Priority ordering

Rank work by how directly it reaches a rung the operator actually runs
on (`ladder.md`):

1. **Chat weight** — `Skill(llm-claim-ledger)`. Ships; runs today.
2. **Repo weight** — the `.kb/` graphs every session reads and writes.
   Ships, but has absorbed the least of {TL, RN}
   (`./claims.kb/repo-weight-rung-is-unbuilt.md`). Highest leverage
   available: the fix lands where the daily work already lives.
3. **Mechanized** — ACS/FP2. Research rungs; they pay out someday, and
   sharpen the ideas meanwhile. Genuine, but not ahead of 1–2.

Skills sharpened here are meant to cash out concretely. A rung that
only proves something is worth less than a rung that removes friction
from tomorrow's sessions.

## Non-goals

- **Novelty as a driver.** Whether the {TL, RN} fusion is a
  contribution (`./questions.kb/is-tl-rn-fusion-novel.md`) is quaternary:
  worth not foreclosing, not worth prioritizing. Prior-art work is
  justified when it changes *our* ontology, not when it establishes
  precedence.
- **Publication-grade scholarship.** Verification is decision-grade —
  enough to settle a design question, no more.
- **Completeness for its own sake.** An unmined chat ledger or an
  unverified prior-art entry is fine standing open indefinitely.

## For agents landing here

This file exists because prior work in this realm was scoped
inside it, and ranked the realm's internal completion above the
operator's productivity. If a task doesn't trace to the ordering above,
say so rather than executing it.
