Obligation is not a stored status: an open claim is obligated exactly
to the extent conclusions rest on it — an importance-weighted
reverse-dependency query over the store. Warranted by operator fiat
(2026-07-24), superseding `obligated(ROUTE)` at every rung: removed
from `Skill(llm-claim-ledger)` the same day; ACS/FP2 prompt executors
inherit the revision (`../prompts.md`). Consequence: routes and
checks are named at discharge or flush, never demanded at claim entry.
