# llm.kb and ACS are complementary halves of one system

llm.kb answers librarianship — naming, discovery, loading,
maintenance — and carries no epistemics in the primitive: no warrant,
no dependency semantics, no revision model beyond newest-file-wins.
ACS answers exactly that half — warrant lattice, dependency cone,
revisions, trust queries — and has no storage story at all. A storage
engine without a consistency model, and a consistency model without a
storage engine.

Evidence: every strong convergence in
`../acs-counterfactual.kb/preservation-audit.md` is llm.kb-side
substrate; every gap is ACS-side semantics. The incumbent's bolt-ons
(status enums, `likelihood`) read as attempts to get semantics out of
physics.
