Nine meta-design criteria, derived by adjudicating the notation against
itself, live at `~/repo/github.com/bukzor/bukzor-agent-skills/
llm-claim-ledger/design.kb/good-smells.kb/` (co-located with the skill
they govern, per `Skill(llm-design-kb)` convention — not duplicated
into this realm). Two organize the rest: writing clarifies thinking
(WC, composing a line forces the judgment it records) and no forced
early decisions (UO, nothing demands a judgment before it's made — `?`
serves both at once). The remaining seven are economies: bare form
stays legal (BF), cheap entry / expensive promotion (CE — the direct
warrant for `claims.kb/obligation-is-derived-not-stored.md`), names
outlive contents (NM — see
`claims.kb/retraction-is-revision-to-tombstone.md`), standing visible
at point of use (SV), meta-operations fit in a breath (SA — `claim
accept: SG` is a complete adjudication), survives dumb media and tools
(DM — the reasoning behind
`claims.kb/sigils-trail-not-lead.md`), and the demo is the spec (DS —
self-demonstrating exemplars outteach policy prose; the direct warrant
for `LC`/`SG` being self-demonstrating lines rather than described
rules).
